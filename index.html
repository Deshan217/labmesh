In community workshops sharing a single congested Wi-Fi connection, dozens of laptops downloading starter kits stall the room. LabMesh streams verified chunks laptop-to-laptop over WebRTC Data Channels. Even when the instructor disconnects, students automatically swap missing chunks peer-to-peer!

⚡ WebRTC Data Channels

Direct peer-to-peer RTCDataChannel with ordered, reliable ArrayBuffer transport. Employs backpressure handling to eliminate congestion.

🛡️ True Decentralized Failover

Seeds even/odd complementary chunks to receivers. When the host leaves, peers automatically swap missing chunks and verify the whole file.

🤖 Google Gemini 3.8 Flash

Grounded live room explanations in Sinhala or English, turning raw WebRTC counts and chunk vectors into plain-language instructor insights.
